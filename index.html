How To Play

🎯 Core Objective

Win 3 sub-boards in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win the main board.

🧩 Board Movement Logic

Your move determines where your opponent plays next! Placing a token in cell #5 sends your opponent to Sub-Board #5.

⚡ Power-Up Arsenal (1 Use Each)

• 💣 Bomb: Destroys any unshielded opponent token in active board.
• ❄️ Freeze: Locks active board for 1 turn.
• 🌀 Wild: Allows move placement in ANY open sub-board.
• 🛡️ Shield: Places a token protected against bombs.

VICTORY!

Player 1 wins the match!
